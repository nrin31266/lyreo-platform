# Animoon portraits

[Animoon by smnandre, revision `7642f8b71609001b17ec15085a194f7ea7ceb1ff`](https://github.com/smnandre/animoon/tree/7642f8b71609001b17ec15085a194f7ea7ceb1ff/images). The bundled WebP portraits are center-cropped and resized to 192 × 192. See ANIMOON-LICENSE (CC0 1.0).

## Botanical portraits

Public domain images from The Met Open Access collection. The images are center-cropped and resized.

- [Erica Fulgida](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/697702) by Pierre Joseph Redouté
- [Climbing Lily](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/697703) by Pierre Joseph Redouté
- [Chrysanthemums](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/57305) by Hokusai School
- [Flowers: Roses and Lilacs](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/337712) by Paul de Longpré
- [Irises](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436528) by Vincent van Gogh
- [Potted Pansies](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/629928) by Henri Fantin-Latour
